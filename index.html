LPS 1175 – NDT

Non-destructive testing (NDT) that gives you confidence in the resistance of your physical security infrastructure.

Security printers and suppliers with vaults, strongrooms, secure stores, production halls and dispatch areas.

A rated door is only as strong as its installation and the response behind it. We carry out non-destructive testing (NDT) of your physical security infrastructure to confirm it will resist attack as intended. We then compare the delay each layer provides with your detection and response times, so you know whether an intruder would be stopped before reaching your materials, data or products.

What we assess

Resistance
Doors, walls, shutters, grilles, enclosures and locks checked against their LPS 1175 security rating, without damaging them.
Delay time
How long each barrier and each layer would hold an attacker using the tools and skills relevant to your threat.
Response time
How long detection, alarm, guard and police response actually take, compared with the delay your barriers provide.
Security layering
Whether perimeter, building, zone and vault layers work together, so no single failure exposes the asset.
Compensating controls
Where a barrier falls short, the extra detection, procedures or reinforcement that close the gap.
Weak points
Hinges, frames, glazing, ducts, cable entries, ceilings and floors that can undermine a rated product.

What you receive

  • On-site NDT inspection of barriers, locks and installations, with photographs
  • Delay-time versus response-time analysis for each protected area
  • Security layering map showing gaps between layers
  • Compensating control recommendations where ratings fall short
  • Prioritised remediation plan for management

What you gain

  • Confidence that your physical security will hold until help arrives
  • Evidence of physical protection ready for customer and certification audits
  • Spending directed to the weakest layer, not the most visible one
