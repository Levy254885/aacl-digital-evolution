About AACL

Audits and Assurance Consult Ltd (AACL Global) is a security, compliance and ISO consultancy with deep roots in security printing, headquartered in Nairobi and working worldwide.

Who we are

Our consultants have led security, compliance and audit functions at security printers, card producers, banks, telecom operators and manufacturers. The same senior practitioners deliver every engagement, onsite or remotely, across Africa, Europe, the UK, the US, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific.

Our values

  • Practical over theoretical: systems field teams actually use.
  • Evidence-based decisions, backed by measurement and benchmarks.
  • Long-term partnership, measured by sustained compliance.
  • Knowledge transfer, so capability stays with you.
  • Executive discretion and confidentiality in every engagement.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with security printers?

Yes. Security printing is our core industry. We support printers and suppliers of ID documents, passports, payment and SIM cards, banknotes, tax stamps, cheques, vouchers, holograms, security inks, papers, plates and software with ISO management systems, PCI Card Production, GSMA SAS, LPS 1175, risk assessment, supplier and internal audits, compliance support and training.

Does AACL issue ISO certificates?

No. AACL is a consultancy. We design and implement your management system, run internal audits and prepare you for the certification audit. Certificates are issued by accredited certification bodies, and scheme certifications by the scheme owners and their appointed auditors.

Which standards do you work with?

PCI Card Production, GSMA SAS (SAS-UP and SAS-SM) and LPS 1175, together with ISO management systems, single or integrated.

Can you work with us remotely?

Yes. We work onsite or remotely. Compliance Support as a Service keeps your systems audit-ready on subscription, with remote internal audits, document control, corrective actions and risk registers.

How do engagements start?

With a conversation about your products, sites and target dates. We then propose a scoped gap assessment, a plan and the engagement model that suits you.

Do you work with suppliers to security printers?

Yes. We support suppliers of security inks, papers and substrates, foils, threads, holograms, inlays, printing plates, production and personalisation machinery, security design software, and secure logistics, storage and destruction services.

How do you handle our confidential information?

Executive discretion is one of our core values. Confidentiality terms are agreed in writing before we review documents or visit your site.

We implement. They certify.

AACL designs, implements and prepares. Certificates are issued only by accredited certification bodies and scheme owners.
